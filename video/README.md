# The demo video

Everything needed to make the CS50x demo video for Draw2Code Web Editor.

- **`cover.png`** is the video's opening card, 1920x1080. It has what the
  CS50 final project spec asks the video to include: the project's title,
  my name, my GitHub and edX usernames, my city, state and country, and
  the date.
- **`cover.html`** is the cover's source. Open it in a browser at
  1920x1080 to see it, or change it and take a new screenshot.

The finished video is on YouTube; its link is at the top of the main
`README.md`.

---

*Use of AI: Claude (Anthropic AI) made the cover and this README at my
direction, from the details I gave it (my name, usernames, location and
date). The video itself is recorded by a separate AI agent, from a prompt I
wrote: it builds a product showcase website with the extension's own tools,
publishes it, hosts the published copy locally and opens it in a browser to
show it works as it does in the editor. The extension it shows is mine.*
